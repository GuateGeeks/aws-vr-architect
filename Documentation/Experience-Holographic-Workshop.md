# Holographic workshop UI: evaluation and redesign

**Scope:** every surface of the AWS Architect Lab (0.17 + the uncommitted voice-access source update), judged against a "Tony Stark workshop" brief and then reworked. The aim is the *style of interaction*, not film trade dress: no Iron Man suit colours, logos, names or character likenesses ship in the app. The assistant stays ATLAS.

Before/after renders: `Validation/BeforeStark/*.png` (0.17 state) vs `Validation/*.png` (this update, regenerated by the PlayMode suite).

## 1. The brief: what makes a "Stark" interface

| # | Principle | What it means in VR |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Light, not slabs** | Surfaces are emitted light: translucent glass, glowing rims, corner brackets. No opaque desktop windows. |
| P2 | **Object-first** | Controls bloom *at the thing you are manipulating* (radial menus, callouts), not on a form two metres away. |
| P3 | **Summon and dismiss** | UI materialises when needed and gets out of the way when not. |
| P4 | **An assistant with presence** | The AI is felt in the room (a core of light that reacts to voice), not only a chat panel. |
| P5 | **Living systems** | Connections visibly carry energy; idle motion says "online". |
| P6 | **Sound as confirmation** | Every hover, press, open and success has a short glassy cue. |
| P7 | **Instrument typography** | Short uppercase labels with wide tracking; one hero element per surface. |
| P8 | **Monochrome plus one accent** | Ice cyan for structure, amber for attention, red only for danger. Brand colours identify; they do not dominate. |

## 2. Evaluation of 0.17

Scores: 1 = absent, 5 = fully there.

| Area | Score | Findings |
|---|---|---|
| Visual language (P1, P8) | 2 | Panels are 98% opaque navy with flat chamfered buttons, so they read as desktop windows. Every button carries orange "- - -" registration marks, which adds noise. The official AWS icons are the most saturated objects in the scene and sit on the holograms like stickers. |
| Information hierarchy | 1 | The command deck shows **20 buttons of equal weight** in four rows. The core flow (Crear → Conectar → Revisar → Desplegar) looks the same as Ordenar or Cargar. The inspector has 10 equal buttons and the Lambda studio 25. |
| Object interaction (P2) | 2 | You select a hologram, then edit it on a panel 2 m away at 29° yaw, so your eyes go back and forth. Ports and grabbing are direct, but everything else lives on the remote panel. |
| Clutter | 2 | `:: MOVER PANEL ::` appears on 6–8 panels at once and is the most repeated text in the scene. Panels overlap: settings and the code studio cover the deck. |
| Assistant presence (P4) | 2 | ATLAS is a 670×450 text panel with a 36 px circle as its avatar, placed over the lab title. The large reactor on the back wall is purely decorative, which wastes the most iconic element in the room. |
| Living systems (P5) | 2 | Links are static thin lines whose boxed "invoca/escribe" labels are heavier than the lines. Flow is visible only during the explicit preview. |
| Motion (P3) | 1 | Panels pop in instantly. Hover only swaps a fill colour. Nothing assembles or confirms. |
| Sound (P6) | 2 | There are three sine beeps (select, success, error) and no hover, open/close or assistant cues. |
| Typography (P7) | 2 | One font (Liberation Sans), mixed case, 14–72 px with no system. Port labels at 14 px cannot be read at table distance on Quest. |
| Hands | 3 | The articulated glove is solid but light grey, which reads as a mannequin, and the fingertips that touch UI carry no light. |
| Safety and honesty | 5 | **Keep:** explicit AWS confirmations, undo everywhere, the "simulation vs AWS" wording, reduced motion, A+X menu recovery, passthrough fallback. |

## 3. What changed (implemented)

### Visual language
- **Glass panels** (`HoloPanelGraphic`): translucent fill (84%) with a cool top gradient, a soft emitted rim (geometry falloff, so no bloom pass on Quest), four corner brackets and an instrument tick ruler. Reading-heavy panels (code studio, keyboards, settings, ATLAS, connection form) use denser 95% "focus glass" and sort above other panels so text behind cannot bleed through.
- **Live buttons** (`LabTarget`): on hover the rim brightens, the brackets open outward and the label shifts toward ice white, eased over about 0.15 s (instant with reduced motion). A tiny tick sounds on aim.
- **Ghost actions:** secondary actions (templates, file row, tool row, *Plegar catálogo*, code line editing, test cases, *Relaciones / Ayuda / Usar ajuste*) have no slab until aimed at, so the lit primary row (*Crear · Definir · Conectar · Revisar · Desplegar* and ATLAS) now leads the deck.
- **Grip bars:** `:: MOVER PANEL ::` is replaced by a quiet `• • • • •` bar that shows **MOVER PANEL** only while aimed at.
- **Instrument typography:** short uppercase labels get wide tracking automatically, but only when they still fit (`LabVisuals.Track`). Port labels go from 14 to 16 px.
- **Palette:** ice cyan `#5CE1F2` for structure, ice white `#C9F6FF` for hover/selection, amber `#FFB24F` for attention, red `#FF6474` for invalid. The orange registration marks remain only on major panels.
- **AWS icons:** the official artwork is left unaltered, as the AWS icon guidelines require. It is framed by holographic registration brackets at 94% opacity, and the badge sits inside a projection cone.

### Object-first interaction
- **Context ring** (`ArchitectureLab.ContextRing.cs`): selecting a hologram blooms a reticle with counter-rotating arcs around the object, plus up to six actions: **CONECTAR** (on the right, where outputs leave), **RELACIONES**, **FICHA**, **CÓDIGO** (Lambda only), **DIAGNÓSTICO** (AWS only) and **QUITAR** (low and apart, undoable). Each action has a leader line to the reticle. The slots avoid the name label and the ENTRADA/SALIDA ports. The ring hides while you connect, hold, place, type, deploy or preview a voice action. It is not a movable menu and calls the same undoable methods as the inspector, which stays the full editor.
- **CONECTAR from the object:** `StartConnectionFrom()` sets the origin directly, so the next selection is the destination.
- **Projection cones:** a faint cone of light rises from each plinth into its hologram, so objects read as projected rather than placed.
- **Selection** is a soft glowing beam ring: ice for selected, cyan for hovered.

### Assistant presence
- **The room reactor is ATLAS:** when ATLAS is active, the back-wall reactor spins up, takes the state colour (cyan listening, amber preview/review, green done, grey paused) and its lens breathes with microphone or speech level. It returns to idle when ATLAS is off and freezes with reduced motion.
- **Compact ATLAS display:** the 36 px circle becomes a miniature reactor (two counter-rotating arc sets around a seven-bar voice waveform). The display moves to the upper-left periphery, tilted toward the user, so it no longer covers the title.
- **Wake chime** plays when ATLAS is activated.

### Living systems, motion and sound
- **Energy beams** (`LabFlow.shader`): data edges are additive soft beams with dashes travelling in the edge direction, tied to the shared reduced-motion clock. Observation (CloudWatch) edges are static and quieter. The pending-connection preview uses the same beam in green, amber or red. Link labels are ghost actions.
- **Materialise, don't pop** (`HoloReveal`): panels fade in behind a bright scan line over 0.32 s. Panels created together are staggered, so the lab assembles itself on start. Reduced motion shows them instantly, and interaction is never blocked.
- **Confirmation pulses** (`HoloPulse`): a shockwave crosses the table on a successful deployment (double, green) and on template load (cyan). A small pulse in the service colour marks a newly placed component. They are skipped with reduced motion.
- **Sound palette** (`LabFeedback`): glass click, hover tick, rising major chime for success, double low pulse for error, open/close sweeps and the ATLAS wake swell. All are synthesised at runtime and rate-limited per cue. `Play(int)` stays compatible.

### Hands
- The glove is now dark titanium, with luminous emitters on the thumb and index tips (the pinch points).

## 4. Constraints honoured
- **Quest 3:** no post-processing, textures or new packages. Glow is mesh falloff and one additive line shader. New per-frame work is limited to one context ring, one presence orb, LabTarget highlight easing (only while changing) and the reactor update.
- **Comfort:** every new motion stops with *Animación: NO*, and pulses and reveals are skipped.
- **Compatibility:** all existing button labels are unchanged, so voice actions, the guide and tests keep working. Ring actions use distinct uppercase labels. The movable-menu count is unchanged. Saved menu poses still apply.
- **Validation:** EditMode **72/72**, PlayMode **53/53**. That includes the new `ContextRingActsOnTheSelectedHologram` test, which checks bloom position, per-kind actions, AWS-only diagnostics, no clipped labels, connect-from-object, remove plus undo, and hiding while holding. The new render is `Validation/38-context-ring.png`.
- **Not yet verified:** headset frame timing with the additional transparent cones and beams, and on-device legibility of the 30 px ring labels. Profile on Quest 3 before the event.

## 5. Next steps (not implemented)
1. **Display font:** add an OFL condensed tech face (for example Rajdhani SemiBold or Saira Semi Condensed) as a TMP font asset for headers and buttons. The network was blocked from this session, so Liberation Sans remains with tracking.
2. **Exploded view:** a two-hand pull-apart on a hologram that fans out its configuration, linked resources and recent events as orbiting cards.
3. **Curved command deck:** split the deck into three panels on a 1.2 m radius arc so every button is equidistant from the user.
4. **Palm bloom:** turn the left-palm quick menu into a radial fan that grows from the palm instead of a 2×2 grid.
5. **Spatial audio:** play UI cues from the touched object's position, and the ATLAS voice from the reactor.
6. **Inspector diet:** move *Relaciones / Ayuda / Quitar* into the ring only, once users are familiar with it, leaving the inspector for name and property values.
7. **Code studio:** group its 25 actions into tabs (Editar · Probar · Publicar) instead of six rows.
